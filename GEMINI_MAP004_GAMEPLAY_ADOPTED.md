# Gemini MAP_004 Crystal Resonance Plan — Adopted Scope

MAP_004 adopts a deterministic Reflection Laser Field, refractor interaction, elite threshold, Archon dais telegraph and Zephyr safe reset. The recommendations for Player ID hash buffs, custom shader uniforms, client-generated cryptographic hash chains and encrypted offline saves are not adopted: they either distort gameplay by player ID or require server/native trust that this browser prototype does not have.

| Gemini recommendation | Implementation |
|---|---|
| Reflection Laser Field | deterministic 70s window in a 300s cycle, 5 damage/sec and 0.72 movement multiplier |
| Prism refractor | interact near refractor clears the active field for its current cycle |
| Prism Golem threshold | harvest 3 shards or defeat 4 gnats |
| Resonance Archon | elite required, interact on a safe dais, 2.6s telegraph then persistent boss presence |
| Mobile visual budget | billboard images and scalar pulse; no shader/particle graph |

> Limit: no completed boss damage, stun-frequency puzzle, drops or haptics are claimed.
